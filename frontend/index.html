Kyoto, Japan

Why I want to visit Kyoto

Kyoto is a beautiful city in Japan known for its temples, shrines, traditional streets, and seasonal scenery.

Top attractions

  • Fushimi Inari Shrine
  • Kiyomizu-dera Temple
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Forest
  • Gion District

Activities to do

  • Visit temples and shrines
  • Try traditional Japanese food
  • Walk through historical streets
  • Take photos during cherry blossom season
